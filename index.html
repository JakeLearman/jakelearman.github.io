About

My name is Jake.

I'm an XR production coordinator at Cinesite in London, with a Computer Science degree and a background in VFX production. I've worked on immersive experiences for FRAMELESS and Printworks Manchester, and on VFX for HBO, Disney+, Netflix, Apple TV+ and A24.

Day to day, that means scoping, scheduling and budgeting projects, and keeping creative, R&D and engineering teams working to the same plan through to delivery.
